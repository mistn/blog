Hi, I'm miuarc. This is my personal blog. I'm currently a first-year graduate student in chemical engineering, with a strong interest in computing.

I mostly write about problems I ran into, study notes, workflow setups, and occasional thoughts on life.

For now it works as a memo pad — I hope these notes will still be useful, or at least interesting, years from now.

The source of this site is on [GitHub](https://github.com/mistn/blog).
