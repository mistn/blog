> [Disroot](https://webmail.disroot.org/) focuses on an open, decentralized web and offers friendly, tracking-free email and collaboration tools. [Autistici](https://www.autistici.org/) insists on independence and decommodification, maintaining secure communications through community self-governance.

## 1. Disroot

> **Storage**: 2 GB; more space requires a donation to Disroot
>
> **Keeping the account**: log in every six months
>
> **Application**: manual review

## 2. Autistici

> **Storage**: unlimited, but please do not abuse it
>
> **Keeping the account**: if you do not log in and read mail for 12 consecutive months, the account is deactivated and everything in it is permanently deleted
>
> **Application**: manual review. Explain your reasons, show that your use is non-commercial, and agree with their anti-surveillance, pro-privacy ethos.
